Privacy Policy

Effective 22 August 2026 · Updated 24 September 2026

The short version. GHOSTFACE is built so that we hold as little about you as possible. We don't ask for your name, phone number or email address. Your in-app messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted — we cannot read them, and neither can anyone who compromises our servers. Your conversations, contacts and call history live on your device, not ours.

The one exception is the optional Ghost Number feature: ordinary SMS text messages travel through the phone network, so they are not end-to-end encrypted and we can read them. Section 3 says exactly what that involves.

This page explains exactly what does reach us, and why.

1. Who we are

Ghostface Limited, a company registered in New Zealand, operates the GHOSTFACE application and this website. Where privacy law applies, Ghostface Limited is the data controller.

Contact: support@ghostface.co.nz

2. What we don't collect

Because these are the questions people actually ask, they come first:

  • No real name. You register with an alias of your choosing.
  • No phone number and no email address are required to create an account or to use the app.
  • No in-app message content. Messages, voice notes, photos, files and calls inside GHOSTFACE are end-to-end encrypted on your device. We relay and briefly store ciphertext we cannot decrypt. (SMS sent through the optional Ghost Number feature are different — see section 3.)
  • No contact list upload. Your contacts are stored on your device and are never sent to us.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no selling of data. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

3. What we do hold

The service cannot function without a small amount of data. This is the whole of it. Rows marked optional exist only if you use that feature.

4. What stays on your device

These never reach our servers at all. They are stored in encrypted storage on your phone, and are lost if you delete the app or trigger a wipe:

  • Your conversations and message history
  • Your contact list
  • Call history
  • Chat wallpapers and per-contact colours
  • Your private keys, including your cryptocurrency wallet keys

5. Encryption

Messages and calls use end-to-end encryption with a hybrid classical and post-quantum handshake — X25519 combined with ML-KEM-768 (NIST FIPS 203) — followed by a Double Ratchet using ChaCha20-Poly1305. Every algorithm is a published public standard; we have not designed or implemented any proprietary cryptography.

Practically, this means the encryption keys exist only on the devices at each end of a conversation. We do not hold them and cannot recover your messages for you, for law enforcement, or for anyone else.

6. Disappearing messages

Messages expire automatically. The timer can be set between 5 seconds and 7 days, and defaults to 1 hour. Expiry is enforced on both devices; undelivered messages also expire from our queue.

7. The wallet

The in-app cryptocurrency wallet is non-custodial. Keys are generated on your device and never transmitted to us. We do not hold your funds, cannot move them, and cannot restore them. If you lose your device and your recovery phrase, the funds are unrecoverable — by us or by anyone.

8. Others who process data for us

  • Hosting — Railway runs our servers and database.
  • Push notifications — Apple (APNs) and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, via Expo's push service) deliver wake-up notifications to your device. The notification itself carries no message content.
  • SMS — Vonage carries Ghost Number text messages to and from the phone network.
  • Payments — plan payments are made in USDC on the public Solana blockchain, which we query to confirm them. Buying cryptocurrency with a card happens on MoonPay's own site, directly between you and MoonPay; we do not receive your card details.
  • Device integrity — on Android, Google Play Integrity confirms the app is genuine.

9. Your choices

  • Delete your account. Use Settings → Danger Zone → WIPE DEVICE (press and hold) in the app: it deletes your data on the device and on our servers in one step. What is deleted, what is kept, and how to ask us instead are set out on ghostface.co.nz/delete-account. Deleting the app alone removes only what is on your device.
  • Notifications. Push notifications can be turned off in your device settings; the app continues to work while open.
  • Access and correction. You can ask what we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Given how little we hold, the answer is usually short.
