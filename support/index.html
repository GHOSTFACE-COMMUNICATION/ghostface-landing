Before you write in

Because of how GHOSTFACE is built, there are some things support genuinely cannot do. It's worth knowing which is which:

  • We cannot read, recover or reset your messages — they're encrypted with keys only your device holds.
  • We cannot recover a lost recovery phrase, PIN, or wallet.
  • We can help with account, alias, subscription and app behaviour questions, and we want to hear about bugs.

Common questions

I lost my device. Can I get my messages back?

No. Message history lives only on your device, encrypted. There is no server-side copy for us to restore, which is the same property that stops anyone else from reading your messages.

If you saved your recovery phrase, you can restore your identity on a new device — your alias and the ability to be reached — but not past conversations.

What is a recovery phrase, and why does it matter so much?

It's a 24-word phrase that encodes a private key. GHOSTFACE uses separate phrases for two different things — your identity and your wallet. They are not interchangeable, and using the wrong one won't work.

Write them down offline. A phrase kept only in a screenshot, a notes app or a cloud backup is as exposed as that service is. Nobody at GHOSTFACE will ever ask you for a recovery phrase — anyone who does is attacking you.

My messages disappeared on their own.

That's the disappearing-message timer working as intended. It's always on and defaults to 1 hour. You can change it per conversation to anything between 5 seconds and 7 days.

Both sides of a conversation see the same timer, and expiry happens on both devices.

Someone can't find my alias.

Aliases are 3–20 characters and may contain only letters, digits and underscores. They're matched exactly, so check for a typo, and note that aliases which look similar are not treated as the same.

The other person also needs to have registered before you can start an encrypted conversation with them.

Calls aren't connecting, or notifications aren't arriving.

Worth checking, in order:

  • Notifications are enabled for GHOSTFACE in your device settings
  • The app has microphone permission (and camera, for video calls)
  • You're not on a network that blocks peer-to-peer connections — some corporate and public Wi-Fi does
  • Both devices are on a recent version of the app

If it still fails, tell us your device model, OS version and roughly when it happened.

Do I need to give a phone number or email?

No. You register with an alias you choose. We don't ask for your name, phone number or email address, and we don't upload your contacts.

What we do hold is set out in the privacy policy — it's a short list.

Is the wallet custodial? Can you recover it?

It's non-custodial. Keys are generated on your device and never sent to us. We can't move your funds, freeze them, or restore them.

If you lose both your device and your wallet recovery phrase, the funds are gone permanently. That's not a policy we can make an exception to — the keys genuinely don't exist anywhere else.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through an app store are managed there — in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings — not in the app.

I want my data deleted.

In the app, go to Settings → Danger Zone → WIPE DEVICE and press and hold. That deletes your account on your phone and on our servers in one step. Deleting the app alone only clears your phone.

What is deleted, what is kept, and how to ask us if you can't open the app: ghostface.co.nz/delete-account.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a vulnerability, please report it privately to support@ghostface.co.nz rather than posting it publicly, and give us a reasonable window to fix it before disclosure. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue.

Reporting abuse

Report abuse to support@ghostface.co.nz. Please note we cannot see message content, so include whatever context you can — the alias involved and what happened.
