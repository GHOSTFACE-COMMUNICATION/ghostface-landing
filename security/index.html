Reporting a vulnerability

If you think you have found a security problem in GHOSTFACE, please email us before telling anyone else. A useful report includes:

  • what you found and why it matters: what an attacker could do;
  • the steps to reproduce it, and the app version, device and OS you used;
  • any proof-of-concept, kept to the minimum needed to show the issue;
  • whether, and how, you would like to be credited.

Please don't include anyone else's personal data in a report.

What's in scope

  • The GHOSTFACE apps for iOS and Android
  • Our API at api.ghostface.co.nz
  • This website, ghostface.co.nz
  • The cryptographic design: handshake, ratchet, call setup, invite cards

Out of scope: denial-of-service, spam or volume testing; social engineering of our staff or users; physical attacks on devices; and services we don't run, such as app stores, push-notification services and our SMS provider (report those to them).

Please don't

  • access, change or delete data that isn't yours. Use your own test accounts;
  • disrupt the service for other people;
  • keep any data you came across beyond what you need to report the issue.

What we commit to

  • We'll acknowledge your report within three business days.
  • We'll keep you updated while we work on it, and tell you when it's fixed.
  • We'll credit you when we disclose it, if you'd like to be.
  • We ask for 90 days to fix an issue before it's made public, or less once a fix has shipped. We'll agree the timing with you.

We won't take legal action against research done in good faith that follows this policy.

Rewards

We don't run a paid bug bounty yet. We do read every report, and we'll say so here if that changes.

Independent review

An independent security review of the GHOSTFACE protocol and selected source code by Cure53 is in progress. We'll publish the report, with its findings and our fixes.
