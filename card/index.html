How this works
  1. This card only works together with a word. The person who sent it will tell you the word separately.
  2. Open the card in GHOSTFACE (or type the code under Messages → Invite → Open a card) and enter the word.
  3. Three wrong words destroys the card. It also self-destructs when the timer runs out.
  4. After you enter the word, the card waits for the sender's phone to come online and confirm it — then the chat opens, verified.

This page sends nothing anywhere: the code lives in the part of the link after #, which your browser never transmits to any server, including ours.
